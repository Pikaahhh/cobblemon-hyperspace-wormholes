    Ultra Space Destinations¶

    Ultra Space is where the wormholes of the Ultra Warp Ride take you. Each wormhole colour leads somewhere different, and most of them have a Pokémon waiting for you.

    Rules In Every Ultra Space World¶

    You can't break or place blocks, use buckets, or use items on blocks (flint and steel, bone meal, hoes and so on). Doors, buttons, riding, throwing Poké Balls and right-clicking Pokémon all still work, and creative players can still build.

    Each world is a set area with an invisible wall around it. The way home is the only way out, on foot or on any mount. Walk or fly into it to go back to where your ride started.

    Pokémon that came out of a wormhole never move from their spot and don't despawn. They're removed once every player has left that world. If you have Waystones installed, you can't use them there.

    The Ultra Space worlds reset every time the world loads, so if you log out inside one you'll be sent home when you log back in.

    Ultra Beasts never spawn on their own anywhere. They only come from wormholes and the Item Analyser.

    Visiting every Ultra Space world (the Ultra Warp tunnel counts too) gives the No Location Untouched advancement.

    Ultra Space Wilds (Red, Green, Yellow, Blue Wormholes)¶

    The Ultra Space Wilds are four worlds, each built 1:1 from the area in Ultra Sun & Ultra Moon. You land in front of the world's exit hole, which is where the way home opens. The wormhole's Pokémon waits as a shadow at the world's hiding place until you come near.

    These are the Pokémon you'll find in each one by default:

    Wormhole World Regular Pokémon Legendaries
    Red Cliff World (floats above a sea of clouds) Swellow, Altaria, Yanmega, Sigilyph, Swanna, Drifblim, Skarmory, Togekiss Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Cresselia, Ho-Oh, Tornadus, Thundurus, Yveltal, Rayquaza, Landorus
    Green Crag World (rocky plain) Nuzleaf, Grumpig, Drapion, Audino, Heliolisk, Golurk, Ferrothorn, Bronzong, Granbull Mewtwo, Cobalion, Terrakion, Virizion, Raikou, Entei, Dialga, Reshiram, Zekrom, Xerneas
    Yellow Cave World Magcargo, Medicham, Hippowdon, Abomasnow, Crustle, Sableye, Steelix, Clefable Regirock, Regice, Registeel, Groudon, Palkia, Heatran, Regigigas, Giratina
    Blue Water World Quagsire, Lombre, Floatzel, Stunfisk, Barbaracle, Jellicent, Azumarill Uxie, Mesprit, Azelf, Lugia, Latias, Latios, Kyogre, Suicune, Kyurem

    Every Wilds Pokémon is level 60. Species your Cobblemon doesn't have yet are skipped. Some Ultra Beasts without a world of their own live in the Wilds too (more on that below).

    These lists come from a pool file for each colour, so a datapack can change them. You can learn more about the pool files here.

    Legendary Chance¶

    The legendary chance depends on the rings and how far you'd flown when you went in. Take the light-years you'd flown, divide by 500 and round down, up to 10. That's the "light-year band" (b) in this table:

    Rings Legendary chance Example at 5,000+ light-years (b = 10)
    0 3% 3%
    1 (b + 2)% 12%
    2 (3b + 4)% 34%
    3 always 100%

    A legendary you don't own yet is 4 times as likely to be picked as one you already own. Some only come if you have certain legendaries in your party:

    Legendary Needs in your party
    Rayquaza Kyogre and Groudon
    Landorus Tornadus and Thundurus
    Suicune Entei and Raikou
    Kyurem Reshiram and Zekrom
    Giratina Dialga and Palkia

    Cosmog, Cosmoem, Solgaleo and Lunala never come from wormholes.

    Server owners can turn wormhole legendaries off with the Wormhole Legendaries setting. The Wilds then only have regular Pokémon. You can learn more about the settings here.

    Shiny Chance (Regular Pokémon)¶

    The longer you've flown, the closer a ringed wormhole gets to its full shiny odds. It reaches them at 5,000 light-years (b = 10). The default chances are:

    Rings Full odds (5,000+ light-years) b = 2 b = 4 b = 6 b = 8
    0 1 in 100 (1%) 1% 1% 1% 1%
    1 1 in 10 (10%) 2% 4% 6% 8%
    2 1 in 5 (20%) 3.1% 7.3% 11.6% 15.8%
    3 1 in 3 (33%) 3.8% 11.2% 18.6% 25.9%

    Under 1,000 light-years (b of 0 or 1) every wormhole has the no-ring odds. Legendaries and Ultra Beasts use Cobblemon's normal shiny rate instead, unless it's changed in the settings. Server owners can change all four odds in the settings, and the chances in between move the same way.

    Alpha Chance¶

    Any wormhole Pokémon can also be an alpha: 2% with no rings, 5% with 1 ring, 8% with 2 and 11% with 3.

    Ultra Beast Worlds (White Wormholes)¶

    White wormholes lead to the Ultra Beast worlds. The wormhole's rings decide which Ultra Beast's world you get. The Ultra Beast stands at its spot in that world, at level 60 with the normal shiny rate.

    Ultra Beast World 0 rings 1 ring 2 rings 3 rings
    Nihilego Ultra Deep Sea 57.7% 1.8% 0.9% 0.9%
    Buzzwole Ultra Jungle 17.3% 2.7% 0.9% 0.9%
    Pheromosa Ultra Desert 16.3% 2.7% 0.9% 0.9%
    Xurkitree Ultra Plant 1.9% 63.4% 17.4% 4.6%
    Kartana Crag World (Wilds) 1.0% 8.9% 21.7% 13.8%
    Celesteela Ultra Crater 1.0% 8.9% 21.7% 13.8%
    Guzzlord Crag World (Wilds) 1.0% 0.9% 13.0% 45.9%
    Necrozma Ultra Megalopolis 1.0% 0.9% 0.9% 0.9%
    Stakataka Crag World (Wilds) 1.0% 3.6% 8.7% 7.3%
    Blacephalon Cave World (Wilds) 1.0% 3.6% 8.7% 7.3%
    Poipole Water World (Wilds) 1.0% 2.7% 5.2% 3.7%

    These assume your Cobblemon has every species. Missing ones are skipped and the rest share their chance. Ultra Beasts that live in the Wilds wait as a shadow, like the Wilds' own Pokémon.

    You never catch Necrozma from a wormhole. A white wormhole to the Ultra Megalopolis starts the Necrozma boss fight instead. After 12 white wormholes in a row without it, your next white wormhole always goes there (until you've beaten Necrozma once). You can learn more about the fight here.

    Each world has its own quirks:

    • Ultra Deep Sea - Nihilego's sealed cave in a world of water. Nothing drowns there and nothing hostile spawns. You walk on the sea floor with lighter gravity.
    • Ultra Jungle - Buzzwole waits down in the jungle and leaps up to its spot when you get close.
    • Ultra Desert - Pheromosa's white sand valley leading to the gem plaza.
    • Ultra Plant - Xurkitree's three towers. Statues of Xurkitree stand all over, and the real one wakes up when lightning strikes it as you come near. Lightning strikes there now and then.
    • Ultra Crater - Celesteela's crater plateau. The giant Celesteela filling the big craters are just for show. The one you can catch is giant too, and it shrinks back to normal size when caught. To battle it, send out a Pokémon while looking at its body. Cobblemon doesn't have Celesteela yet, so you'll need a mod that adds it, like Cobblemon: Myths and Legends Compat.
    • Ultra Megalopolis - the top of the Megalo Tower, alone in space. You can only walk around up there. It's where you fight Necrozma.

    Purple Wormholes¶

    A purple wormhole drops you in the sky near a structure in your world, with a way home behind you. If you're riding a flying Pokémon you stay in the air. If not, you land on the ground below. No Pokémon waits at a purple wormhole.

    More rings mean rarer structures. If you have Dungeons and Taverns or Legendary Monuments installed, purple wormholes can take you to their structures too:

    • No rings - villages and Cobblemon structures.
    • 1 ring - vanilla structures, and Dungeons and Taverns taverns.
    • 2 rings - large Dungeons and Taverns structures, and Legendary Monuments sites without a legendary.
    • 3 rings - Legendary Monuments shrines and the Dungeons and Taverns End Castle.

    If it can't find one of that kind, it uses a lower tier.

    Waystones: Warp Stone Rides¶

    If you have Waystones installed, your legend can fly you to your waystones.

    1. Give your legend a Warp Stone to hold.
    2. Open a wormhole and fly into it like normal.
    3. The tunnel has one wormhole for each of your activated waystones, coloured by the waystone's dimension. Fly into the one you want.

    After those comes one green "uncharted" wormhole that leads somewhere far off in your world.
