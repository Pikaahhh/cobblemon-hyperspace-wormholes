    Rewards¶

    Hyperspace¶

    Source Reward
    Each finished task 1 EXP Candy for every player on the island (XS at ★, S at ★★, M at ★★★, L at ★★★★, XL at ★★★★★)
    All 3 tasks A Golden Floating Poké Ball for each player (once)
    Legendary mission Enchanted Golden Apple and 3 EXP Candy for every player on the island
    Floating Poké Ball 1-3 rolls of the loot below, plus money if you have CobbleDollars
    Golden Floating Poké Ball 1-3 rolls of the golden loot below
    Legendary (5 star trips) The legendary itself, if you catch it
    Mega Evolved Pokémon (4 star trips) Its Mega Stone, for every player in the battle
    Wild Pokémon Caught at a set level for the trip's stars. You can learn more here.

    Floating Poké Ball Loot¶

    Each roll picks one entry. Then the item counts change with the trip's stars, from x0.2 at ★ up to the full amount at ★★★★★, but never below 1. Big Haul gives you extra rolls, and Item Power can switch a normal ball to the golden loot.

    Entry Amount Normal ball weight Golden ball weight
    Each of the 18 Hyper Berries 1-3 70 each 280 each
    Rare Candy 1 252 1008
    EXP Candy (size by stars, see below) 1-4 378 1512
    Each of 21 Cobblemon Poké Balls (Beast, Cherish, Dive, Dream, Dusk, Fast, Friend, Heal, Heavy, Level, Love, Lure, Luxury, Moon, Nest, Net, Premier, Quick, Repeat, Sport, Timer) 1 12 each 48 each
    Gem by stars: Coal (★), Emerald (★★), Diamond (★★★ to ★★★★★) 4-12 189 756
    Ingot by stars: Copper (★), Iron (★★), Gold (★★★ to ★★★★★) 4-12 189 756
    Golden Apple 1-5 - 126 (★) to 630 (★★★★★)
    Each of Enchanted Golden Apple, Master Ball, Mega Stone, Blank Z, Max Mushroom (the last three from Mega Showdown) 1 - 126 (★) to 630 (★★★★★) each

    The EXP Candy size goes by stars: XS at ★ and ★★, S at ★★ and ★★★, M at ★★★ and ★★★★, L at ★★★★ and ★★★★★, XL at ★★★★★.

    CobbleDollars¶

    With CobbleDollars installed, Floating Poké Balls give you money too. It's 2,500 per ball and 10,000 per golden ball, times stars/5, so a normal ball gives 500 to 2,500.

    Modpacks can change this loot with a datapack. You can learn more about that here.

    Wormholes¶

    Source Reward
    Item Analyser A Pokémon of the gem's type (level 45, 65 or 80), 5% an Ultra Beast (level 75)
    Item Analyser with a full Cosmic Jar Cosmog (level 5)
    Ultra Space Wilds A level 60 Pokémon from that world, sometimes a legendary
    Ultra Beast worlds A level 60 Ultra Beast
    Purple wormholes A trip to a structure (rarer ones with more rings, Legendary Monuments shrines at 3 rings)

    Bosses¶

    Beating Hoopa Unbound gets each player a Hoopa Confined (level 70) and the Hoopa: Unbound advancement. Anyone who never had a Prison Bottle gets one too.

    Beating Necrozma gets each player in the party a Necrozma (level 70) and the Light Recaptured advancement.
