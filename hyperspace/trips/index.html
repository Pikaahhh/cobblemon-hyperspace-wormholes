    Inside Hyperspace¶

    The Island¶

    Every trip gets its own Hyperspace island. It's a copy of the land around the distortion you went in at, floating in Hyperspace with a grey edge. You'll arrive in front of the way home, a portal set into a wall of the island.

    You can't break or place blocks in Hyperspace, not even in creative mode, and buckets don't work either. A server can have up to 64 islands in use at once.

    Time Limit¶

    The action bar shows your Time Remaining. When it hits zero you're sent back to where you went in. Splice trips show "Time Remaining: ???" instead.

    To leave early, you simply need to walk into the way home. You can't walk straight back into it until you've stepped out of it once. Dying ends the trip too, and so does /stuck. You can learn more about commands here.

    Your sent-out Pokémon are put back in their balls before every teleport.

    Levels¶

    Wild Pokémon levels and the level of the Pokémon you catch both depend on the distortion's stars:

    Stars Wild Pokémon level Level of Pokémon you catch
    ★ 101-120 13-19
    ★★ 121-135 15-25
    ★★★ 136-150 20-35
    ★★★★ 151-165 35-43
    ★★★★★ 166-185 50-56

    While you're in Hyperspace, any party Pokémon below the trip's range is raised to a random level in that range. It goes back to its real level and EXP when you leave. Your Pokémon don't gain EXP in Hyperspace, and you can't use EXP Candy there.

    A Pokémon you catch is set to the "caught" level in the table (legendaries are at least level 80). The mod raises Cobblemon's max level to 200, but outside Hyperspace Pokémon still stop gaining EXP at level 100.

    Which Pokémon Spawn¶

    Cobblemon's normal spawning is turned off in Hyperspace. Each trip gets its own small pool instead. It's taken from the hyperspace Pokémon tag (866 species by default) and picked by the distortion's types and stars. At 1 star you only get first-stage Pokémon. At 2 stars it's middle stages and Pokémon that don't evolve, and from 3 stars up it's fully evolved ones. Baby Pokémon never spawn.

    The pool has 8 species, sorted by base stat total:

    Rarity Species in the pool Base stat total Chance per spawn
    Common 3 up to 340 60%
    Uncommon 2 341-460 28%
    Rare 2 461-540 10%
    Ultra-rare 1 541+ 2%

    Up to 9 wild Pokémon stay around you (within 32 blocks), and new ones fill the gaps. Encounter Power raises that number.

    A rare or ultra-rare spawn is a herd 10% of the time. That's a big alpha leader at the top of the level range, holding its type's gem. It comes with 3 to 5 of its pre-evolutions.

    The alpha chance is 5%, more with Alpha Power. The shiny chance is your trip's shiny rolls at Cobblemon's shiny rate. You can learn more about Alpha Power and Sparkling Power here.

    Legendary And Mega Bosses¶

    A 5 star distortion has a 5% chance to hold a legendary. Everyone who went through the portal gets their own copy at the middle of the island. A 4 star trip has a 10% chance of a Mega Evolved Pokémon spawning near you, rolled for each player as they enter.

    When one is waiting you'll hear its cry with an echo, and you'll get an advancement as you enter ("A Legendary Encounter" or "Untapped Power").

    The legendary is at the trip's wild level, twice its normal size and with double HP. Its ability is Hyperspace Guard, so it can't be knocked out and you have to catch it. It has the normal shiny chance, better with your shiny rolls. Only the player it belongs to can fight it, and other players can't see it. Catching it gets you the Pokémon, back in its normal form, plus the legendary mission reward. Its ability changes to a normal one when you catch it.

    While your own legendary is waiting or alive, normal Pokémon don't spawn for you.

    The Mega is level 200, also double size and HP, with the ability Hyperspace Mega (doubles its HP). Its shiny chance is normal. You can't catch it, but anyone can fight it, and beating it gives its Mega Stone to each player in the battle.

    The legendaries come from the legendaries Pokémon tag. Only species your Cobblemon has that can walk or fly are used: Kyogre, Groudon, Deoxys, Tornadus, Thundurus, Landorus, Zygarde, Volcanion, Tapu Koko, Tapu Lele, Tapu Bulu, Tapu Fini, Zeraora, Kubfu, Zarude, Okidogi, Munkidori, Fezandipiti, Ogerpon and Terapagos. If one of them has the distortion's type, it gets picked. Kyogre and Groudon show up in their Primal form and go back to normal when caught.

    The Megas can be any Mega form from Mega Showdown, and it tries to pick one with the trip's type.

    Tasks¶

    Every island has 3 tasks, shown at the top left of your screen (they're hidden during battles). Everyone on the island shares them. The tasks can be:

    • Defeat 3 Pokémon.
    • Catch 3 Pokémon.
    • Destroy 3 Hyperspace Poké Balls.
    • Quick battle and defeat 5 Pokémon.

    Each finished task gives every player on the island 1 EXP Candy: XS at 1 star, S at 2, M at 3, L at 4 and XL at 5. Finish all three and each player gets a Golden Floating Poké Ball on the island (once per player) and the Taskmaster advancement.

    If the island has a legendary, there's an extra mission, "Catch or defeat the legendary Pokémon.". It gives an Enchanted Golden Apple and 3 EXP Candy.

    Floating Poké Balls¶

    Each island has 6 Floating Poké Balls. You can't punch them. You'll need to send out a Pokémon and hit them with one of its moves. You can learn more about how to do that here.

    A normal ball drops 1 to 3 rolls of the Floating Poké Ball loot, and a golden ball drops the golden loot. Item counts go up with the trip's stars, with full amounts at 5 stars. You can learn more about the loot here.

    If you have CobbleDollars installed, each ball also gives you money: 2,500 for a normal ball or 10,000 for a golden one, times the stars out of 5. So a normal ball gives 500 at 1 star, up to 2,500 at 5 stars.

    Pop all 6 on an island (between any players) for the Professional Popper advancement.

    Music¶

    Hyperspace has its own music, and it keeps playing through battles. If you have Reactive Music installed, it gets turned down while you're in Hyperspace.
