    Donuts¶

    Donuts are what you feed Hoopa at a spatial distortion to open it, like in Legends: Z-A. The donut sets how long the trip lasts and gives it Flavor Powers. Everyone who goes through that portal gets the same powers.

    You can only get Hoopa by beating the Hoopa Unbound event.

    Cooking¶

    Donuts are cooked in Cobblemon's Campfire Pot, in two steps:

    1. Make a Plain Donut from Wheat, Wheat, Egg and Sugar (in any order).
    2. Put the Plain Donut back in the pot with 1 to 8 berries around it to get a berry donut.

    Any berries work, so there isn't only one way to make them. These are a few of them:

    Each recipe makes one donut. A donut that already has berries can't go back in the pot, and the seasoning slots aren't used for donuts.

    You can use any of Cobblemon's Cheri, Chesto, Pecha, Rawst, Aspear, Oran, Persim, Lum, Sitrus, Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot, Hondew, Grepa and Tamato berries, the 18 type-resist berries (Occa to Roseli), and the 18 Hyper Berries from Hyperspace.

    Hold Shift over a berry to see what it adds to a donut. The "Donut Info" shows its calories, flavor score and which powers it gives.

    Names And Stars¶

    A donut is named after the berry it has the most of and its strongest flavour, followed by its stars, like "Roseli Berry Meringue Donut ★★". Each flavour gives a different name:

    • Sweet - Meringue
    • Spicy - Curry
    • Sour - Jam
    • Bitter - Chocolate
    • Dry - Cream

    If flavours tie for strongest, you get a Rainbow donut.

    The stars come from the donut's flavour score, which is all its berries' flavour points added up:

    Flavour score Stars
    120 ★
    240 ★★
    350 ★★★
    700 ★★★★
    960 ★★★★★

    You'll need Hyper Berries for 3 stars and up. The best normal berries have 40 flavour points, so 8 of them only get you to 320 (2 stars). Hyper Berries have 4 times the flavour of their normal berry and a lot more calories. They drop from Floating Poké Balls in Hyperspace.

    Calories And Trip Time¶

    Your donut's tooltip shows its Calories. That's its berries' calories added up, then raised by 10% per donut star. So a 5 star donut has 1.5 times its berries' calories.

    At a distortion, the calories buy you time at that distortion's rate. 1 calorie is 1 second at a 1 star distortion:

    Distortion Calories per second
    ★ 1
    ★★ 1.6
    ★★★ 3.5
    ★★★★ 7.5
    ★★★★★ 10

    If your donut has one star fewer than the distortion, it burns 1.5 times as fast. Two or more stars fewer and it burns 2.5 times as fast. A trip always lasts at least 1 minute 30 seconds.

    Here are some examples, in minutes:seconds of trip time:

    Donut Score Stars Calories At ★ At ★★ At ★★★ At ★★★★ At ★★★★★
    Plain Donut 0 - 0 1:30 1:30 1:30 1:30 1:30
    8 Oran 320 ★★ 576 9:36 6:00 1:50 1:30 1:30
    8 Babiri 280 ★★ 672 11:12 7:00 2:08 1:30 1:30
    3 Hyper Babiri 420 ★★★ 1560 26:00 16:15 7:26 2:19 1:30
    5 Hyper Babiri 700 ★★★★ 2800 46:40 29:10 13:20 6:13 3:07
    8 Hyper Babiri 1120 ★★★★★ 4800 80:00 50:00 22:51 10:40 8:00

    Flavor Powers¶

    Powers only come from the donut's strongest flavour. If two flavours tie for strongest, both give their powers. The number of points in that flavour sets the power level. Hold Shift over a donut to see its powers.

    Strongest flavour Lv. 1 Lv. 2 Lv. 3 Powers
    Sweet 120 200 300 Alpha Power, plus Humungo (5+ berries) or Teensy (4 or fewer berries)
    Sweet 420 525 700 Sparkling Power replaces Alpha Power
    Sour 120 200 300 Item Power and Big Haul
    Dry 120 200 300 Encounter Power and Catching Power
    Spicy, Bitter - - - No powers (they still count towards stars)

    Here's what each power does in Hyperspace at its default strength:

    Power Lv. 1 Lv. 2 Lv. 3
    Sparkling Power (shiny odds) +1 shiny roll +2 shiny rolls +3 shiny rolls
    Alpha Power (alpha odds; base 5%) 10% alpha 20% alpha 50% alpha
    Humungo Power (bigger Pokémon) size 1.0x to 1.5x 1.13x to 1.5x 1.38x to 1.5x
    Teensy Power (smaller Pokémon) size 0.5x to 1.0x 0.5x to 0.87x 0.5x to 0.62x
    Item Power (Floating Poké Ball rolls golden-ball loot) 25% 50% 100%
    Big Haul (extra items per Floating Poké Ball) +2 +3 +5
    Encounter Power (more Pokémon around you; normally 9) +3 +6 +9
    Catching Power (a failed Poké Ball throw catches anyway) 10% 20% 30%

    Without Sparkling Power a trip has 1 shiny roll. Each roll is one chance at Cobblemon's shiny rate, so 4 rolls is about 4 times the normal shiny chance. Server owners can make each power stronger or weaker, or turn it off, in the Settings.

    A Distortion Splicer trip has no donut, so it doesn't get any powers.
