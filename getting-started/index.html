    Getting Started¶

    Cobblemon: Hyperspace & Wormholes has a few dependencies you'll need to install for this mod to work:

    • Fabric API
    • Fabric Language Kotlin
    • Cobblemon
    • Mega Showdown
    • AllTheMons x Mega Showdown

    Once you have all of those mods installed, there are also some optional mods that H&W supports:

    • Mod Menu - opens the H&W settings screen.
    • Xaero's Minimap & World Map, or JourneyMap - spatial distortions show up on your map.
    • Waystones - give your legend a Warp Stone and it can fly you to your waystones.
    • CobbleDollars - Floating Poké Balls give you money when you pop them.
    • Legendary Monuments - purple wormholes can take you to its shrines.
    • Dungeons and Taverns - more places for purple wormholes to take you.
    • Reactive Music - turned down while Hyperspace music is playing.
    • CobbleNav - shows which Pokémon you can find on your Hyperspace trip.

    Accessing Hyperspace¶

    You can access Hyperspace with Hoopa or Distortion Splicers.

    With Distortion Splicers:

    1. Craft Distortion Splicers with the recipe.
    2. Locate a Hyperspace Distortion. Can find easier with Xaero's Minimap & Worldmap installed.
    3. Right-Click distortion with the splicers.
    4. Walk into distortion.

    With Hoopa:

    1. Cook up a donut.
    2. Locate a Hyperspace Distortion.
    3. Send out your Hoopa, then feed it a donut.
    4. Walk into distortion.

    Warning

    Opened Distortions remain open for 15s. Players need to enter it during that time otherwise the item is wasted. Distortion Splicers set a random allocated time inside the Hyperspace between 90s and 5 minutes. If you don't leave quick enough, your armour will lose a lot of durability and you'll take a bit of damage too.

    Distortions only show up in the Overworld, on cliffs and other walls 64 to 160 blocks away from you. You can learn more about finding them here.

    Inside Hyperspace¶

    Hyperspace has some quirks to it, such as your Pokémon's levels increasing above Level 100. The level changes are temporary and revert to normal upon exiting.

    Tasks¶

    You'll notice some tasks on the top left of your screen. These tasks reward you with EXP Candy, as well as some other items if you finish them all and pop the Golden Poké Ball.

    Floating Poké Balls¶

    There are Floating Poké Balls scattered across each Hyperspace. Popping them can reward you with many different items. You'll need to send out a Pokémon and use moves on them by pressing down middle click to pop them.

    When you are finished with that Hyperspace, you simply need to return to the distortion and walk through it.

    You can learn more about Quick Battles, Floating Poké Balls and other quirks of Hyperspace here.

    Accessing Ultra Space¶

    Ultra Space is a bit more difficult to access, requiring a machine early on. You can learn about accessing Ultra Space in more detail here, otherwise here is a basic rundown:

    1. Build an Item Analyser by putting a Cobblemon Data Monitor on top of a Fossil Analyzer.
    2. Craft an Empty Jar and keep it in your inventory. Using the Item Analyser can fill it up with cosmic energy.
    3. Once the jar is full, put it in the Item Analyser with a Golden Apple and charge the machine with lightning or an Electric move. Out comes Cosmog!
    4. Raise Cosmog into Solgaleo or Lunala.
    5. Fly on your Solgaleo or Lunala and press V to open a wormhole, then fly into it to start the Ultra Warp Ride.

    Coloured wormholes take you to the Ultra Space Wilds, white ones to the Ultra Beast worlds and purple ones to a rare structure in your world.

    Bosses¶

    If you keep splicing distortions you'll eventually run into Hoopa Unbound, and if you keep going through white wormholes you'll find Necrozma. Beat them and you get to catch your own.
