# Cobblemon: Hyperspace & Wormholes

Cobblemon: Hyperspace & Wormholes aims to introduce the Ultra Warp Ride from Pokemon Ultra Sun & Ultra Moon as well as Hyperspace from the recent Pokemon Legends: Z-A DLC.

# Hyperspace

Hyperspace can be accessed through Spatial Distortions across the world.

- **Distortion Splicers:** Early on, you'll use these to access Hyperspace, as Hoopa won't be available right away.
- **Donuts:** Donuts can be fed to Hoopa to open Spatial Distortions with different effects and an increased duration.
- **Stars:** Higher star distortions allow for better encounters. Who knows, maybe you'll end up finding something legendary.
- **Bring your friends!** Your friends can enter the same Hyperspace with you if they make it in time. Certain encounters let every player catch their own copy of that Pokemon. You'll probably want to bring a few for a special encounter too…
- **Tasks:** Each Hyperspace instance has tasks for players to complete, rewarding EXP Candy and other useful items.
- **Quick Battles:** Send out a Pokemon and use attacks without needing to enter a battle directly. Useful for quickly knocking out Pokemon and taking out Floating Pokeballs.

# Wormholes

Wormholes are the other side of this mod, introducing the Ultra Warp Ride. You'll need a Solgaleo, Lunala or a fused form of Necrozma.

- **New Locations:** Travel through Ultra Space and visit the homes of different Ultra Beasts and other Pokemon.
- **Rare Encounters:** Like the real games, you can encounter Legendary Pokemon inside some dimensions. You might also find something you aren't really ready for…

# Progression

This mod introduces its own method of getting Hoopa and Cosmog.

- **Cosmog:** Cosmog is obtained by setting up a device to create Wormholes on demand. These Wormholes differ from the ones found in Ultra Space as they only summon Pokemon. Each time one is opened, and each time you ride through a wormhole, a jar you're carrying has a chance to collect Cosmic Energy, until you can eventually summon a Cosmog of your own!
- **Hoopa:** With enough Distortion Splicer uses, you'll eventually find a way to capture Hoopa. That's all I can say.

- [Fabric Language Kotlin](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-language-kotlin)
- [Cobblemon](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon) 1.8.4
- [Cobblemon: Mega Showdown](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-mega-showdown)
- [AllTheMons x Mega Showdown](https://modrinth.com/mod/allthemons-x-mega-showdown-legacy)

This mod is for Fabric 1.21.1, and needs to be installed on both the client and the server.

# Works with

- [CobbleNav](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-pokenav): Shows available Pokemon inside a Hyperspace instance.
- [CobbleDollars](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobbledollars): Floating Pokeballs grant money when popped.
- [Legendary Monuments](https://modrinth.com/mod/legendary-monuments): Some Wormholes can lead to Legendary Monument structures.
- [Xaero's Minimap](https://modrinth.com/mod/xaeros-minimap) and [Xaero's World Map](https://modrinth.com/mod/xaeros-world-map): Spatial Distortions show up on your maps.
- [Waystones](https://modrinth.com/mod/waystones): Give your Warp Ride Pokemon a Warp Stone and travel to your activated waystones in style.
