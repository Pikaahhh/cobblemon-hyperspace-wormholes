# Cobblemon: Hyperspace & Wormholes

Hyperspace & Wormholes brings two features from the newer Pokemon games into Cobblemon: **Hyperspace** from Pokemon
Legends: Z-A, and the **Ultra Warp Ride** from Pokemon Ultra Sun & Ultra Moon.

---

## Hyperspace

Hyperspace can be accessed by feeding Hoopa donuts.

**Donuts:** donuts are cooked in Cobblemon's Campfire Pot, and the seasonings you add will change what your trip is
like. Berries, golden food, ores and a few other things all do something different, so it's worth experimenting.

**Stars:** the better your donut, the stronger Hyperspace gets, and the better the rewards. Who knows, maybe you'll
find something legendary waiting for you.

**Bring your friends:** anyone who follows you through the portal will join you on the same trip.

**Tasks:** every trip comes with a few things to do, with rewards for finishing them.

**Quick battles:** your Pokemon can use their moves outside of battle. Keep an eye out for Floating Poke Balls too!

---

## Ultra Wormholes

If you're riding a **Solgaleo**, **Lunala** or one of Necrozma's fused forms, you can press **V** to have it roar open a
wormhole. Flying into it will start the Ultra Warp Ride.

Once inside Ultra Space, you get to choose where you end up, whether that be in the Overworld or a whole other
dimension.

**No legend yet?** Try playing a special flute and see who answers the call.

**Keep looking up:** wormholes don't only open when you go looking for them.

## Requirements

- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
- [Fabric Language Kotlin](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-language-kotlin)
- [Cobblemon](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon) 1.8+
- [Mega Showdown](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-mega-showdown)

This mod is for Fabric 1.21.1, and needs to be installed on both the client and the server.

## Works with

- [**CobbleNav**](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-pokenav): shows which Pokemon can be found on your Hyperspace trip
- [**CobbleDollars**](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobbledollars): Floating Poke Balls give money when popped
- [**Legendary Monuments**](https://modrinth.com/mod/legendary-monuments): some wormholes might lead somewhere familiar
- [**Waystones**](https://modrinth.com/mod/waystones): your waystones can be reached through wormholes too

## Datapacks

The Pokemon lists (Hyperspace spawns, legendaries, wormhole Pokemon, who takes donuts) are Pokemon tags, and the
floating Poke Ball loot is a loot table, so modpacks can swap them out. There's an example of each in
[`demo-datapack`](demo-datapack/hyperspace-wormholes-demo).
