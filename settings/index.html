    Settings¶

    What Most People Change¶

    • Shiny Odds - for more (or fewer) shiny Pokémon from wormholes, change the odds for 0, 1, 2 and 3 rings. Going from 100 to 50 on Shiny Odds: No Rings doubles the no-ring chance from 1% to 2%.
    • Legendaries - each way of getting legendaries has its own switch under Legendaries. With Wormhole Legendaries off, Wilds wormholes only lead to regular Pokémon.
    • Legendaries only from Legendary Monuments - turn Hyperspace Legendaries and Wormhole Legendaries off, and keep the Legendary Monuments setting on Monuments.
    • Data Monitor Griefing - turn it off if you don't want Data Monitors breaking.
    • Max Distortions Per Player - raise it to see more spatial distortions around. At 4 you keep up to 4 around you in busy areas.
    • Distortion Minutes - raise it to have distortions stay around for longer.
    • Keep Distortions Away From Bases - turn it on if you don't want distortions showing up where you live.
    • Distortion Star Weight - raise a rank's weight to make it more common, for example 5 Star from 5 to 20.
    • Total Distortions for Hoopa Boss and Total White Wormholes for Necrozma Boss - lower them to find Hoopa Unbound or Necrozma sooner, for example from 12 to 6.

    Which Pokémon come out of each wormhole colour isn't a setting, you change that with a datapack. You can learn more about that here.

    The Settings Screen¶

    On Fabric, install Mod Menu and open the mod's config button in your mod list to get the Hyperspace & Wormholes Settings screen. On NeoForge you don't need Mod Menu. Open Mods from the title screen or pause menu, pick H&W and press Config. Every setting has a tooltip that explains it and gives an example, so just hover its name. Reset to Defaults puts everything back.

    The screen edits config/hyperspace_wormholes_gameplay.json. On a server, the server's own settings file is used. The server sends its settings to every player who joins, so changing them in your own game only changes your single-player worlds.

    Bosses¶

    Setting Default What it does
    Total Distortions for Hoopa Boss 12 How many splices in a row without Hoopa before the next one is guaranteed. 0 turns pity off.
    Total White Wormholes for Necrozma Boss 12 How many white wormholes in a row without Necrozma before the next one is guaranteed. 0 turns pity off.
    Enable Hard Mode? Off On: bosses are always in hard mode. Off: a boss is only in hard mode for players who have beaten it.

    Wormholes¶

    Setting Default What it does
    Shiny Odds: No Rings 1 in 100 The shiny chance of a regular Pokémon past a wormhole with no rings, and of regular Item Analyser Pokémon.
    Shiny Odds: 1 Ring 1 in 10 The shiny chance with 1 ring, after flying 5,000 light-years or more.
    Shiny Odds: 2 Rings 1 in 5 The same with 2 rings (20%).
    Shiny Odds: 3 Rings 1 in 3 The same with 3 rings (33%).
    Legendary Shiny Odds 0 The shiny chance of legendary, mythical and paradox Pokémon past a wormhole or from the Item Analyser. 0 uses Cobblemon's own shiny rate.
    Ultra Beast Shiny Odds 0 The same for Ultra Beasts.
    Data Monitor Griefing On Whether lightning and Pokémon moves can break Data Monitors. It shows "Custom" if the file has the three switches set differently.

    The shiny odds are written as "1 in N", so 100 is a 1% chance, 10 is 10%, 2 is 50%, 1 is always and 0 is never. The ring odds are what you get after a long ride. Below 5,000 light-years, a wormhole with rings has a chance between the no-ring odds and its own. It goes up every 500 light-years, like in Ultra Sun & Ultra Moon. You can find that table here. Legendaries and Ultra Beasts have their own two settings, which use Cobblemon's normal shiny rate unless you change them.

    Updating from 2.0.2 or older

    Older versions had Base Shiny Chance (a percent) and Multiplier Per Ring. The first time 2.1.0 starts, it works out the four odds from them and writes the new ones to the file. That way, odds you changed carry over. A file still on the old defaults gets the new defaults (2 and 3 rings were 1 in 5.26 and 1 in 2.78, now 1 in 5 and 1 in 3).

    Legendaries¶

    Setting Default What it does
    Hyperspace Legendaries On Whether a legendary can appear on a Hyperspace trip (the 5% on 5-star distortions). Megas still come.
    Wormhole Legendaries On Whether a legendary can wait past a red, green, yellow or blue wormhole in the Ultra Space Wilds. Ultra Beasts aren't affected.
    Catch Beaten Bosses On Whether you can catch Hoopa and Necrozma after beating them. Off: the boss leaves once it's beaten, but Hoopa's Prison Bottle still drops.
    Cosmog Summon On Whether the Item Analyser summons Cosmog from a full Jar of Cosmic Energy and a Golden Apple. Off: you can't get Solgaleo and Lunala, the Ultra Warp Ride mounts, this way.
    Legendary Monuments Monuments Only does something when Legendary Monuments is installed. Monuments: the legendaries it has shrines for are left out of Hyperspace. Both: they stay in.

    Operator commands that ask for a set legendary (/hyperspace createDistortion legendary=..., /wormhole create) still work with these off.

    If you only want legendaries from Legendary Monuments, turn Hyperspace Legendaries and Wormhole Legendaries off and keep Legendary Monuments on Monuments. Leave Catch Beaten Bosses on if you still want to catch Hoopa and Necrozma, and leave Cosmog Summon on, since that's how you get a mount for the Ultra Warp Ride.

    Hyperspace¶

    Setting Default What it does
    Max Distortions Per Player 2 How many distortions a player keeps around them in the busier half of the map (half that, rounded up, in the rest). 0 to 16.
    Distortion Minutes 60 How many minutes a distortion stays before it disappears. Ones already there keep their time. 1 to 10080.
    Keep Distortions Away From Bases Off On: new distortions don't appear near where online players respawn or in chunks players have spent an hour around. Off: they can appear anywhere.
    Distance From Bases 128 How many blocks from online players' respawn points distortions keep when the setting above is on. 0 to 1000.
    Legendary Shiny Odds 0 The shiny chance of a Hyperspace legendary. Shiny rolls from donuts multiply it. 0 uses Cobblemon's own shiny rate.
    Distortion Star Weight: 1 Star ... 5 Star 35, 28, 20, 12, 5 How common each distortion rank is compared with the others.

    Donut Power¶

    These set how strong each Flavor Power is. 1 is normal, 2 is twice as strong and 0 turns that power off. They all default to 1.

    Setting Default
    Sparkling Power (Shiny Odds) 1
    Alpha Power (Alpha Odds) 1
    Humungo Power (Bigger Pokémon) 1
    Teensy Power (Smaller Pokémon) 1
    Item Power (Better Loot) 1
    Big Haul (More Loot) 1
    Encounter Power (More Pokémon) 1
    Catching Power (Second Catch) 1

    Settings Only In The File¶

    config/hyperspace_wormholes_gameplay.json has everything on the screen. The shiny odds are under wormholeShinyOneIn, and the Monuments setting is legendaryMonuments ("monuments" or "both"). Some of the others are:

    Key Default Setting
    distortionMinutes 60 Distortion Minutes
    distortionsAvoidBases false Keep Distortions Away From Bases
    distortionBaseDistance 128 Distance From Bases

    It also has a few things the screen doesn't show:

    Key Default What it does
    wormholeRingWeights [1, 1, 1, 1] Multiplies the odds of a wormhole having 0, 1, 2 and 3 rings.
    monitorBreaksFromLightning true Lightning can burn out the Data Monitor (90% chance).
    monitorBreaksFromElectricMoves true Electric moves can break the Data Monitor (power / 150, at most 90%).
    monitorBreaksFromOtherMoves true Other moves always break the Data Monitor.

    Other Config Files¶

    These are plain files for server owners and modpack makers.

    config/hyperspace_wormholes.json:

    Key Default What it does
    requireMount true A wormhole ride needs one of the allowed mounts.
    allowedMounts Solgaleo, Lunala, Necrozma The Pokémon that can open wormholes. Necrozma only counts as Dusk Mane or Dawn Wings.
    gameWormholeColours true The game's wormhole colours (red, green, yellow, blue to the Ultra Space Wilds). false uses the mod's older colours.

    config/hyperspace_wormholes_boss.json has defaults for boss fights that the boss data doesn't set itself, like catchSeconds (180) and emptyHealSeconds (120, a boss nobody fights for that long heals to full).

    config/horizons_movefx.json is for the Solgaleo, Lunala and Necrozma signature move animations:

    Key Default Where What it does
    cameraShake true your game Camera shake during the move animations
