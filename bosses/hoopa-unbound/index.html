    Hoopa Unbound¶

    The Hoopa Unbound event is a boss fight inside Hyperspace. Beating it is the only way to get Hoopa, and it gives you a Prison Bottle too.

    Finding Hoopa Unbound¶

    Hoopa Unbound shows up when you open distortions with a Distortion Splicer. Each splice has a 5% chance to be the event. Donut and Hoopa portals never lead to it. Neither do distortions an admin made with a set legendary or Mega.

    After 12 splices in a row without it (counted for the player using the splicer), your next splice is always the event. This pity stops once you've beaten Hoopa Unbound.

    Everyone who walks into that portal is part of the event. You can learn more about finding and opening distortions here.

    What Happens¶

    1. You arrive on a special arena island (plains hills around a flat floor) instead of a copy of your world. At first it seems normal. The clock counts down and the tasks are up, but there are no wild Pokémon and the way home is small and can't be used.
    2. After about 10 seconds the clock and the task list turn into glitch text, the music tears apart and the HUD disappears.
    3. A giant ring opens in the sky and Hoopa Unbound comes down through it.
    4. Right-click Hoopa to get ready. The fight starts when everyone on the island who can battle has done that.

    The trip timer stops during the event, and no wild Pokémon spawn on the island for as long as it lasts.

    The Fight¶

    Hoopa Unbound is level 90 and knows Hyperspace Fury, Psychic, Dark Pulse, Focus Blast, Gunk Shot and Nasty Plot. It has 10x its normal HP per player and starts with +1 in its highest stat. If you're alone (nobody else entered that Hyperspace) you get 2 Pokémon out at once, otherwise 1.

    You can learn more about the shared battle, banned moves and hard mode here.

    At 80% HP the battles pause. Hoopa backs off and throws two rings. Volcanion and Zygarde (level 60) come out and take its place in every player's battle. At 40% HP it throws three: Dialga, Origin Forme Giratina and Palkia (level 80).

    • You still only have your usual number of Pokémon out, against all the called legendaries at once.
    • Each called legendary has one HP pool shared by all players (its normal HP times the number of players). Knock one out in one battle and it faints in all of them.
    • Beating them doesn't hurt Hoopa. Once they're all down, Hoopa comes back into the same battles with +1 in its second-lowest stat.
    • A hit big enough to go past both marks goes straight to the second wave, and a hit that beats Hoopa skips any waves that are left.

    Winning¶

    Every battle ends and Hoopa turns back into Hoopa Confined (level 70) with the Prison Bottle effect. Each player gets their own Hoopa to catch. Throw any Poké Ball at it. You have about 3 minutes.

    If you've never had a Prison Bottle (Mega Showdown's item), one is left lying on the floor for you. Only you can pick yours up, and you only ever get one.

    Server owners can turn catching off with the Catch Beaten Bosses setting. Hoopa then just leaves, but the Prison Bottle still drops.

    Everyone on the island is marked as having beaten Hoopa Unbound, so no more pity and hard mode next time. Then Hoopa leaves, the sky ring closes and the way home opens. There's no time limit. You also get the Hoopa: Unbound advancement.

    Losing Or Leaving¶

    If nobody on the island can battle any more, the event ends the same way but without a Hoopa to catch.

    Warning

    Using a Distortion Splicer during the event ends it. Hoopa leaves and everyone is pulled towards the way home, harder every second. This still uses one of the splicer's uses.

    After the event the task list and the music stay off until you leave Hyperspace.
