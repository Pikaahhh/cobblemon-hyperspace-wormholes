    How Boss Fights Work¶

    H&W has two boss fights: Hoopa Unbound in Hyperspace and Necrozma on the Megalo Tower. They both work the same way.

    Starting A Fight¶

    Up to 4 players can fight a boss together.

    1. Right-click the boss to get ready.
    2. Once every player there who can battle has right-clicked it, a screen transition plays.
    3. Everyone is moved to their spot in front of the boss and the fight starts.

    If you're already in a battle you'll get "You're already in a battle.". If you have no healthy Pokémon, you'll get "You have no Pokémon able to battle."

    One Shared Battle¶

    Each player has their own normal Cobblemon battle, but they all count as one fight.

    The boss has one shared HP pool: its normal max HP times 10 per player (so 10x solo, 20x with two, 30x with three and 40x with four). The HP bar at the top shows its name, level, HP, stat changes and status.

    Each round waits for everyone to pick a move ("Waiting for all moves to be selected…"). The boss attacks once per round, going for whichever player's Pokémon it can hurt the most. Moves that hit all foes, like Earthquake, hit every player's Pokémon. Everyone sees everyone's moves in their battle log.

    The boss uses Cobblemon's strongest battle AI and starts with +1 in its highest stat. At some HP marks it changes form or calls in help (each boss's page has the details). A big hit can skip past a mark. If nobody fights it for 2 minutes it heals back to full.

    Banned Moves¶

    Some moves are greyed out against bosses ("This move cannot be used in this battle."):

    Fissure, Sheer Cold, Guillotine, Horn Drill, Endeavor, Pain Split, Final Gambit, Super Fang, Nature's Madness, Ruination, Guardian of Alola, Destiny Bond, Perish Song.

    One-hit KO moves from other mods are blocked too. If one gets through anyway (for example through Sleep Talk or Metronome), it doesn't work on the boss.

    Winning And Catching¶

    A boss can't faint. When it gets to 1 HP it's beaten, the battles end and each player gets their own copy to catch. Throw any Poké Ball at yours and it's caught. You can't see other players' copies.

    Server owners can turn catching off with the Catch Beaten Bosses setting. The boss then just leaves once it's beaten, but Hoopa's Prison Bottle still drops.

    Hard Mode¶

    If the player who starts the fight has beaten that boss before, it's in hard mode. Then it's level 100, it holds its type's boosting item (for example Twisted Spoon for a Psychic type), and its "+1 in its highest stat" goes to every stat instead.

    Server owners can make hard mode always on with the Enable Hard Mode? setting.

    Pity¶

    Each boss has a pity count. After 12 tries without meeting it, the next try always finds it. Pity stops once you've beaten that boss.

    For Hoopa Unbound, every Distortion Splicer splice you open counts as a try. For Necrozma it's every white wormhole you enter.

    Server owners can change the number or turn pity off. You can learn more about the boss settings here.
